## English (U.S.)

**Name:** PearchHA

**Subtitle:** Home Assistant in your menu bar

**Promotional text:** Keep rooms, controls and history one click away on your Mac.

**Keywords:** home assistant,smart home,menu bar,rooms,automation,home

**Description:**

PearchHA puts the rooms, values, history and controls you care about into a native macOS menu-bar app. Connect to your own Home Assistant instance, choose the useful entities and keep a quick check from turning into a dashboard session.

PearchHA keeps your Home Assistant connection on your Mac and talks directly to the instance you select. Use focused history, readable rooms and capability-aware controls for the small actions that belong between everything else.

## App review answers
