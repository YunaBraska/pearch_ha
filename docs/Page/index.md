<section class="section">
  <h2>A calmer way to check the house.</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">PearchHA talks to your own Home Assistant instance, remembers its session in Keychain and keeps the most useful state where your attention already is.</p>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature"><h3>Make rooms readable</h3><p>Group entities by room and choose the values, gauges, labels and units that make a quick glance useful.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>See the change, not just the number</h3><p>Open focused history previews and move from the last reading to a longer time range when something needs attention.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Act from the right place</h3><p>Use capability-aware controls and service actions without rebuilding a separate dashboard for every small task.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
